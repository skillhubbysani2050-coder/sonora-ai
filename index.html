Good to know

Questions,
answered plainly.

Is Sonora a working product or a prototype?

A prototype. Voice playback runs in your browser with voices already installed on your device; there is no hosted text-to-speech service or account yet. Try the studio.

Can I use the API today?

Not yet. The Python snippet is illustrative; a hosted Sonora API is not connected. The scripted agent and the studio are the parts you can try now.

Where does my audio go?

Sonora never receives your text. Speech is generated by your browser: most device voices run locally, but a few, such as Chrome's Google voices, are synthesised online by the browser maker. The full explanation.

What does Sonora cost?

Nothing is for sale on this page. Pricing is not configured, and no plan or charge is offered. See what changed recently.
